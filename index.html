About the Consortium

From a Regional Group to a National Network

Formerly the MEG Texas Consortium

The MEG Translational Research Consortium (MEG-TREC) grew out of the MEG Texas Consortium. It was renamed to reflect its national expansion and its growing role in magnetoencephalography research, clinical applications, and technology development.

Each year the consortium holds a meeting that brings together clinicians, researchers, and industry professionals for keynote lectures, panel presentations, research discussions, and networking. Registration has been free, with an RSVP required.

Focus Areas

Where MEG Makes a Difference

Topics represented at consortium meetings

Epilepsy

Localizing interictal and ictal activity to guide presurgical planning.

Neurodevelopment

Studying how brain networks form and change across childhood.

Aging and Neurodegeneration

Investigating network changes that may signal disease before symptoms appear.

Functional Mapping

Identifying sensory, motor, and language cortex before neurosurgery.

Brain Computer Interfaces

Using the temporal resolution of MEG to decode brain activity.

MEG Technology

New sensors, wearable systems, and advanced analysis methods.
