2026

4th Annual MEG-TREC Meeting

June 5, 2026 · Memphis, Tennessee

Host: Le Bonheur Children's Hospital Neuroscience Institute, Faculty Office Building. Organizing committee chaired by Roozbeh Rezaie, PhD.

The 2026 meeting brought together clinicians, researchers, and industry professionals with keynote addresses and panel presentations on current trends in clinical and translational MEG research. Topics emphasized applications in epilepsy, neurodevelopment, aging, and neurodegenerative disease, together with advances in MEG technology. Registration was free with an RSVP.

Source: Le Bonheur Children's Hospital meeting page

2025

MEG-TREC 2025

April 25 and 26, 2025 · Gainesville, Florida

Host: University of Florida MEG Lab, at the Norman Fixel Institute for Neurological Diseases. Organized by Abbas Babajani-Feremi, PhD.

Participants came from across North America. Andrew Papanicolaou, PhD, opened the meeting with a keynote on 50 years of MEG history. Sessions covered MEG connectivity, biomarker research, early detection of brain disease, and the integration of MEG with EEG and imaging. Continuing medical education credit was offered.

Discussions at the meeting led to new partnerships between academia and industry focused on data sharing, advanced analytics, and validating MEG biomarkers for regulatory approval.

Sources: UF Health Fixel Institute, Highlights from MEG-TREC 2025; MEG-TREC 2025 meeting site
